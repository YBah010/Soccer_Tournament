Louisville & Indiana Youth Soccer Tournament

Bringing young people from across Louisville and the Indiana region together through soccer, friendship, fun, and networking.

Soccer Tournament Application

Yaya's Family Foundation invites young soccer players from Louisville and surrounding Indiana communities to apply to participate in our community soccer tournament. The goal of the tournament is to create an opportunity for young people to compete, have fun, build friendships, and network with other members of the community.
